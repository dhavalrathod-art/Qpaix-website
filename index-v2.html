FAQ

Questions we hear often

How much does a software project cost? +

It depends on features and complexity. After a free discovery call we send a clear written estimate, either a fixed price or a monthly team cost, so you know the budget before we start.

How long will it take to build my app? +

A simple app or website usually takes 4–8 weeks. Larger platforms are built in phases, so you can launch a first version early and add features over time.

Will I own the source code? +

Yes. You get full ownership of the code, designs and data. We sign an NDA before the project starts.

Do you provide support after launch? +

Yes. Every project includes a free support period after go-live, and we offer monthly maintenance plans for updates, monitoring and new features.

Can you work with our existing software or team? +

Absolutely. We regularly improve, integrate with or take over existing systems, and our developers can join your in-house team as dedicated resources.
