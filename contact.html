Common questions

Still unsure? Just send the form with your question. There's no wrong question.

Is the first consultation really free? +

Yes. The first 30-minute call is completely free and comes with no obligation. We use it to understand your needs and tell you honestly if and how we can help.

I don't have a technical team. Can you still help? +

Absolutely. Most of our clients are business owners, not engineers. We explain everything in simple language and handle all the technical work for you.

Will my idea stay confidential? +

Yes. We're happy to sign an NDA before you share details. Just tick the NDA box in the form and we'll send one across first.

Do you work with clients outside Ahmedabad? +

Yes, we work with businesses across India. Most meetings happen on video calls, and we visit on site whenever the project needs it.
